
# Dhan Sathi Bank — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account Information
- Check your balance anytime through our app, net banking, or AI assistant.
- Minimum balance for Savings A/C: ₹1,000 (Basic), ₹5,000 (Silver), ₹25,000 (Gold), ₹1,00,000 (Platinum).
- Account statements are available for download in PDF format.

## Fixed Deposit (FD)
- Minimum FD amount: ₹10,000.
- Interest rates: 6.5% – 7.5% p.a. depending on tenure and amount.
- Premature withdrawal allowed with 1% penalty on interest.

## Loan & EMI
- Personal loan up to ₹25 lakh at 10.5% p.a.
- Home loan up to ₹1 crore at 8.5% p.a.
- EMI auto-debit from your linked savings account on the 5th of each month.

## Card Services
- Lost/stolen card: call 1800-999-8888 immediately for blocking.
- Credit card bill payment due by 15th of each month.
- Reward points redeemable on partner websites.

## Working Hours
- Branch banking: Monday to Friday, 10 AM – 4 PM; Saturday 10 AM – 1 PM.
- AI assistant: available 24/7.
- Phone banking: 1800-999-8888 (24/7).

## Fraud & Security
- Never share your OTP, PIN, or password with anyone, including bank staff.
- If you suspect unauthorized transactions, contact us immediately for account freeze.
