# ConnectIndia Mobile — Frequently Asked Questions

## Prepaid Plans
- ₹199 plan: 1.5 GB/day, unlimited calls, 28 days validity.
- ₹299 plan: 2 GB/day, unlimited calls, 28 days validity.
- ₹599 plan: 3 GB/day, unlimited calls, 56 days validity.
- ₹999 plan: unlimited data (fair usage 3 GB/day), 84 days.
- All plans include 100 SMS/day.

## Postpaid Plans
- ₹499 plan: 75 GB/month, unlimited calls, Disney+ Hotstar.
- ₹999 plan: 150 GB/month, unlimited calls, Netflix Basic, Amazon Prime.
- Bill generated on 1st of each month, due by 15th.

## Data & Network
- 5G available in 50+ cities. Your device must support 5G.
- If experiencing slow speeds, try toggling airplane mode or restarting your device.
- Fair usage policy applies after daily limit on prepaid plans.

## SIM & Porting
- New SIM activation takes up to 4 hours.
- Port your number to ConnectIndia: SMS PORT <your number> to 1900.
- Porting takes 3–7 business days.

## Recharge & Payment
- Recharge via app, website, UPI, or retail stores.
- Auto-recharge available for prepaid users via linked UPI/card.
- Postpaid bills can be paid via net banking, UPI, or auto-debit.

## Working Hours
- AI support: 24/7.
- Store visits: 10 AM – 8 PM, all days.
- Customer care: 198 (toll-free from ConnectIndia numbers).
