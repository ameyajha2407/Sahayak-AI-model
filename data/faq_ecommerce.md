# ShopEasy India — Frequently Asked Questions

## Return Policy
- Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- Product must be unused and in its original packaging.
- Electronics have a 7-day replacement guarantee if defective.
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item is received.

## Delivery Information
- Standard delivery: 3–7 business days depending on your location.
- Express delivery (metro cities): 1–2 business days at ₹99 extra.
- Free delivery on orders above ₹499.
- You will receive SMS and email updates at each delivery stage.

## Cancellation Policy
- Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped (status: "placed").
- Once shipped, the order cannot be cancelled — please use the return process instead.
- Refund for cancelled orders is credited within 3–5 business days.

## Payment Options
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
- Credit/Debit Cards
- Net Banking
- Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders under ₹10,000
- EMI available on orders above ₹3,000

## Working Hours
- Customer support is available 24/7 through our AI assistant.
- Human agents are available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM – 9 PM IST.
- For urgent issues outside business hours, our AI agent will create a priority ticket.
